Buddy Connections

A life full of people and purpose.

Aging can quietly shrink a person’s world. Friends move away, driving becomes harder, and the places that once felt easy to reach can feel distant. Buddy Connections is how we keep that world open — our monthly social gatherings that bring older adults together, hosted by Tamara and Justine.

We believe people flourish when they are seen, included, and needed. Connection brings laughter into the week and gives everyone something meaningful to anticipate.

What Happens at a Gathering

Every month looks a little different — that’s the fun of it. Gatherings might include:

  • Coffee mornings and brunch
  • Afternoon tea
  • Games and card afternoons
  • Crafts and creative projects
  • Gardening together
  • Music and sing-alongs
  • Birthday and holiday celebrations
  • Seasonal outings to favorite local places
  • Good conversation with new friends
  • Something to look forward to, every month

Purpose Projects

Our Buddies still have gifts to give — and giving feels good. Some gatherings roll up our sleeves for someone else:

  • Care packages for neighbors and friends
  • Encouragement cards
  • Church outreach projects
  • Food-pantry support

Who Comes

Buddy Connections gatherings are part of package life at Blessing Buddy — package families receive first invitation to every gathering, and their Buddy (Tamara or Justine) is right there with them, so nobody walks into a room of strangers alone. Curious whether a gathering could be a good first step for your loved one? Ask us at your family consultation and we’ll tell you what’s coming up.
