Monthly Buddy Events

Coming soon.

Aging can quietly shrink a person’s world. Friends move away, driving becomes harder, and the places that once felt easy to reach can feel distant. Monthly Buddy Events are how we keep that world open — social gatherings that bring our Buddies together once a month, hosted by Tamara and Justine. Think of them as our Connection pillar, in real life.

We believe people flourish when they are seen, included, and needed. Connection brings laughter into the week and gives everyone something meaningful to anticipate.

What Happens at a Gathering

Every month looks a little different — that’s the fun of it. Gatherings might include:

  • Coffee mornings and brunch
  • Afternoon tea
  • Games and card afternoons
  • Crafts and creative projects
  • Gardening together
  • Music and sing-alongs
  • Birthday and holiday celebrations
  • Seasonal outings to favorite local places
  • Good conversation with new friends
  • Something to look forward to, every month

Purpose Projects

Our Buddies still have gifts to give — and giving feels good. Some gatherings roll up our sleeves for someone else:

  • Care packages for neighbors and friends
  • Encouragement cards
  • Church outreach projects
  • Food-pantry support

What It Costs

Monthly Buddy Events are separate from visit packages. Each event will carry a per-person fee that covers the venue, food, and activities — we’ll announce the fee when each event is scheduled, so you’ll always know the cost before you sign up.

Who Comes

When Monthly Buddy Events launch, package families will receive first invitation to every event, and their Buddy (Tamara or Justine) will be right there with them, so nobody walks into a room of strangers alone. Curious whether an event could be a good first step for your loved one? Ask us at your family consultation and we’ll tell you what’s in the works.
