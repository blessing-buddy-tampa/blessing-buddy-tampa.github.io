Frequently Asked Questions

The questions every family asks us — answered plainly.

How much does it cost?

Every package is one set, fixed cost, tailored to your loved one’s rhythm at your free consultation — no surprises, and no obligation. Packages are month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime.

Is the family consultation really free?

Yes — completely free. We meet, learn about your loved one, answer your questions, and confirm we’re the right fit. If we’re not, we’ll say so honestly and point you the right way.

Who will actually visit my loved one?

One of us — Tamara or Justine. Blessing Buddy is not an agency, and we don’t send rotating strangers. Your loved one gets a familiar face, on a consistent schedule.

Are you registered, background-screened, and insured?

Yes. We are a registered Florida Homemaker & Companion service (AHCA File #39981334), we are Level 2 background-screened, and we are insured.

What days and times do you visit?

For now, visits run on weekdays between 9 AM and 2 PM. As families ask for them, we may add evenings and weekends in time.

How will I know how the visit went?

After every visit, we send the family notes with update texts — plus pictures and videos from the visit — so you can see how your loved one is doing, not just take our word for it.

Do you have experience with dementia or hearing loss?

Yes — with our own grandparents. Our grandpa is 95 and living with dementia, and hearing loss is part of our family’s everyday life, too. We are not specialists or medical experts by any means, but we have empathy, patience, and a real understanding of how difficult it can be — for them, and for you.

Can you drive my loved one to appointments?

Not yet. For now, we can meet your loved one at the appointment — waiting-room company, note-taking with permission, and a family update afterward. If the need is there, driving is something we may add in the future.

What happens if there’s an emergency during a visit?

We call 911 first, and then we call you. We don’t provide emergency response as a service — but your loved one will never be left alone in a crisis.

Something breaks at home — can you help?

Usually, yes. We have basic handyman experience and will do our best to tackle the issue first. If a professional is needed, we can pull from our trusted contacts and handle the scheduling for you.

How do payments work?

Packages are billed monthly — and we can set up automatic monthly drafting, so it’s one less thing on your plate.

What’s the smallest way to start?

You don’t have to commit to a package. Our Quick Buddy Check-In is a one-off 30-minute visit — book one whenever you’d like an extra visit or a first meeting, no package required. Many families start there.

Do the Monthly Buddy Events cost extra?

Yes — events are separate from visit packages and carry a per-person fee covering the venue, food, and activities. We announce the fee when each event is scheduled, so you always know the cost before you sign up.

Can we pause or cancel?

Anytime. Visits reschedule free with 24 hours’ notice, and packages are month-to-month — cancel effective at the end of the current month, with no fee, and pause for travel or a hospital stay whenever life requires it.
