Visit Packages

A familiar face. A meaningful connection. Every week.

Flat-fee visits and packages to meet your loved one’s unique needs. Laundry, dog walking, and household help during a visit are all included — no upcharges, ever. Most families choose a weekly package:

PackageWeekly Schedule
Weekly ConnectionOne 1-hour visit every week
Twice-Weekly ConnectionTuesday and Thursday
Three-Times-WeeklyMonday, Wednesday and Friday
Weekday ConnectionMonday through Friday
Quick Buddy Check-InOne 30-minute visit; for package families

Flexible by design — no long-term contract, no 4-hour minimum. Services can be adjusted up or down as your loved one’s needs change, and your family’s exact package is tailored together at your consultation.

Every package includes a consistent weekly schedule; visits personally provided by Tamara or Justine; personalized companionship and light household help during the visit; family updates with permission; priority access to additional weekday visits and Buddy events; and connections to trusted local resources when needed.

Add-on services — appointment accompaniment, errands, outings, moving support, and special projects — are booked separately at the same simple flat fee.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Life happens. Visits can be cancelled or rescheduled free with 24 hours’ notice; inside 24 hours, the visit counts as used — illness and family emergencies are always the exception, just call us. Packages are month-to-month: cancel anytime, effective at the end of the current month, with no cancellation fee — and you can pause for travel or a hospital stay and restart when you’re ready. For moving-day bookings, please give 48 hours’ notice, as we block the whole day for your family.
