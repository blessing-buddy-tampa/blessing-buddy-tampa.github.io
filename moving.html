Moving & Transition Services

From the first box to feeling at home. Moving to — or from — a facility is one of the hardest days a family faces. You don’t have to carry it alone. We stand beside your family through the whole move, at our same set, fixed cost — no surprises.

Moves We Help With

  • Moving into independent living, assisted living, or memory care
  • Moving out of a facility — back home, or closer to family
  • Returning home after a hospital or rehab stay
  • Downsizing from the family home to a smaller home or condo
  • Moving into a family member’s home — or an in-law suite (see our ADU & In-Law Suites page)
  • Preparing a home for sale after a move

How We Help

  • Downsizing & sorting — patient, unhurried help deciding what goes, what’s gifted to family, and what’s donated
  • Packing support — treasures packed with care and clearly labeled
  • Coordination — movers, the facility, and family kept on one calm schedule
  • Move-day companionship — a familiar, friendly face with your loved one while the logistics swirl
  • Unpacking & home setup — bed made, photos on the wall, kitchen and closet arranged, TV and devices set up, so the new place feels like home on day one
  • Settling-in visits — regular Buddy visits after the move, so a new community quickly starts to feel familiar
  • The old home — donation and sale coordination, and preparing the home for its next season, with staging and real estate support through our sister company, Styled Spaces, and the appropriate licensed businesses
